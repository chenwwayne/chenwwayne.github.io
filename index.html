Short Bio

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Engineering at the Faculty of Engineering and Quantity Surveying, INTI International University, Nilai, Malaysia, and a full-time researcher at the School of Information and Communication Technology, Shenzhen City Polytechnic.

My research focuses on efficient artificial intelligence, lightweight neural network architectures for edge devices, and practical edge-computing solutions. I am particularly interested in making modern AI models more accurate, efficient, and deployable in real-world settings, including vision-language models and on-device intelligence.

Before joining Shenzhen City Polytechnic, I worked on AI algorithm performance optimization at Xiaomi and researched image-algorithm applications for TFT-LCD defect detection at TCL Industrial Research Institute. I also work on research and curriculum development in AI mobile applications.
